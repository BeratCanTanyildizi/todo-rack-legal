Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Todo Rack ("we", "our", or "us") is a team task management application. This Privacy Policy describes what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo URL from Google Sign-In.
  • Task data: Task titles, descriptions, assignments, comments, and completion status you create within the app are stored in Firebase Firestore.
  • Device token: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) collects a push notification device token to deliver task notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and display your profile within your team.
  • To assign, update, and track tasks shared within your team.
  • To send push notifications when tasks are assigned to you or completed.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is shared only with:

  • Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Messaging, Cloud Functions) — for app functionality and data storage.
  • Members of your team — task data and your display name/avatar are visible to teammates within the app.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can delete your account in the app: open Hesap (Account) and choose Hesabi sil. This removes your account, your team memberships, and tasks you created. You may also request deletion at the support page below.

5. Children's Privacy

Todo Rack is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Security

We use Firebase Security Rules so that only members of a team can read that team's tasks. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.
